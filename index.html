        CONNECT IDE, GITLAB DUO & GIT HOOKS
        
      

      

        
Intercept and purge silent collateral code mutations right inside your editor before committing to Git.


        

          
1. Zero-Install Instant Audit

          
Whenever Cursor, Claude, or Copilot edits your code, run this 1-line command in your project terminal with your prompt:

          

            npx scopeguard "Add dark mode toggle to header"
            
          

        


        

          
2. What Happens Automatically

          
ScopeGuard diffs your uncommitted Git working tree against HEAD, isolates unrequested collateral deletions or style mutations, and automatically pops open your local Surgical Workbench in your browser to purge threats with 1 click.

        


        

          
3. Optional: Automatic Git Pre-Commit Guard

          
Block silent AI regressions from ever reaching your GitHub repo by staging ScopeGuard in your git hooks:

          

            npx scopeguard --install-hook
